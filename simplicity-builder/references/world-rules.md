# Career-to-world interpretation

Translate career information into meaningful **places to explore**, while preserving real-world facts in dialogs. The user chooses the creative setting; examples are illustrations, not a finite menu.

- Career roles -> major regions of a chosen world (planets, castles, studios, sailing vessels, libraries, arenas, etc.)
- Projects -> journeys, missions, exhibits, murals, artifacts, attractions.
- Skills -> constellations, guilds, instruments, toolboxes, collections.
- Education -> academy, observatory, origin place, research station.
- Achievements -> milestones, discoveries, trophies, featured installations.
- Personal introduction -> origin story, visitor's welcome or curator's note.

A user prompt about a floating city may suggest districts, airships and cloud bridges; a prompt about a music festival may suggest headline stages and backstage tents. The AI should make an **original** mapping rather than simply renaming a castle.

Structure: one evocative title, subtitle grounded in the person's profession, 1–8 accessible landmarks with meaningful entry details, optional user-approved professional links, concise summary and readable mobile layout. Do not fabricate accomplishments, metrics or employers to fill a fictional theme.

**Visual differentiation:** vary color palette, `world.scene_svg` scene composition, symbols, world title, narrative and regions. Starter presets are convenience fallbacks only. Keep dark readable backgrounds; `world.scene_svg` is optional but recommended outside starter presets. No external assets needed.

**Privacy:** generated site contains no telemetry or remote calls. The host AI provider may process résumé content according to its own policies. Never publish without explicit approval. Include no home address, phone, email or unapproved personal link.
